Plan Your Adventure

Prepare properly before heading outdoors.

🎒 Equipment Checklist

  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Water bottle
  • First-aid kit
  • Backpack
  • Weather-appropriate clothes
  • Torch and extra batteries
  • Tent and sleeping bag
  • Food and snacks

⚠️ Safety Guidelines

  • Follow your guide's instructions.
  • Check weather conditions before departure.
  • Carry sufficient drinking water.
  • Stay with your trekking group.
  • Follow marked trails.
  • Keep emergency contacts available.
  • Inform someone about your route.
  • Follow local park and campsite rules.

🗓️ Sample Trip Schedule

  • 6:00 AM: Meet at starting point
  • 7:00 AM: Safety briefing
  • 8:00 AM: Trek begins
  • 12:00 PM: Lunch break
  • 2:00 PM: Reach campsite
  • 4:00 PM: Set up camp
  • 7:00 PM: Dinner and rest
